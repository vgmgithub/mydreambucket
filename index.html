🎯 Bucket List Plan

Turn “someday” into a date

Every dream with a cost and a year becomes a savings goal. Tell the app what you can put aside a month, and it shows which dreams fit on time — and which move a little later.

  1. 1
    What it will cost by thenToday’s price grows with prices (about 6% a year — you can change it).
  2. 2
    Where the money waitsThe nearer the date, the safer the place:
Under 1 yearSavings account, RD or a liquid fund~6.5%
1–3 yearsFD, RD or a short-term debt fund~7%
3–5 yearsA conservative or balanced hybrid fund~8.5%
5+ yearsAn equity index fund, by monthly SIP~10%
  1. 3
    What you have put aside countsIt grows too, and comes off what is still needed.
  2. 4
    One amount a monthMust-dos first, then the soonest — money frees up as each dream is reached.

Careful assumptions, not promises — kinds of places to keep money, never named products. Amounts in ₹, $, AED, S$, NZ$ or Sri Lankan Rs.

Questions

Good to know before you go

Is My Dream Bucket free?

Yes. There is no account to make, nothing to sign up for and nothing to pay.

Where is my list kept?

On your phone only. Your dreams, places, memories and photos never leave it — there is no server holding them and nothing is sent to us.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes — once installed, the app opens and works offline. The Places map is the one part that needs the internet, to load the map pictures and the outlines of countries and states.

How do I move my list to a new phone?

Menu → Back up my list saves a file with everything, photos included. On the new phone, install the app and choose Restore from a backup.

I keep my bucket list in MyNotes. Can I bring it?

Yes. Menu → Bring my list from MyNotes. On the same phone and web address it is read straight from MyNotes; anywhere else, pick a MyNotes backup file. MyNotes itself is never changed.

Can my family have their own lists?

Yes — one list per person, each with its own emoji and colour. The Bucket List Plan brings everyone’s dreams with a cost into one plan.

Is it on the Play Store or the App Store?

You install it straight from this page — it works on Android phones, iPhones, iPads and computers, and opens full screen like any other app.

Which currencies can I use?

Indian Rupee, US Dollar, UAE Dirham, Singapore Dollar, New Zealand Dollar and Sri Lankan Rupee — change it any time from the menu.
